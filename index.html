Privacy Policy

Last Updated: [15-09-2026]

1. Introduction

Nova Nexus ("Nova Nexus," "we," "us," or "our") operates a centralized identity and single sign-on ("SSO") platform that authenticates users across the Nova Nexus ecosystem of affiliated applications (each a "Side Website"). This Privacy Policy explains what limited information we collect, why we collect it, how it is stored and protected, and how it is used to recognize you across the ecosystem. By creating an account, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

Email address — used for account identification, verification, and essential service communication.
Username — a display name you choose (or, if you sign in with Google, one derived from your Google profile), shown to you and, where relevant, to Side Websites you access.
Password — if you register directly with Nova Nexus, your password is never stored by us in readable form; it is handled entirely by Firebase Authentication using industry-standard salted hashing. If you use Google Sign-In instead, we never see or store a password at all.
Google profile data — if you choose "Continue with Google," Google shares your name, email address, and (if available) profile photo with us for the purpose of creating and recognizing your account, subject to Google's own privacy practices for that exchange.
Authentication metadata — a unique user identifier, account creation timestamp, sign-in provider, and email verification status.
Basic technical and device data, collected automatically by our infrastructure providers (Firebase/Google Cloud, and Google reCAPTCHA for bot protection — see Section 6) for security and abuse-prevention purposes. We do not independently log or analyze this data for marketing purposes.

We do not collect your physical address, phone number, or payment details, unless you voluntarily provide such information to a specific Side Website, which is then governed by that Side Website's own privacy practices.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to: create and secure your account; verify your email address (or recognize Google's own verification of it); authenticate you when you sign in; generate short-lived, cryptographically signed tokens that let you move between Side Websites without re-entering your credentials; display your username and email on your dashboard; detect and prevent fraudulent or unauthorized account activity; and send essential account communications, such as verification links and password reset emails. We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not build behavioral or marketing profiles from it.

4. The Nova Nexus Ecosystem & Single Sign-On

Nova Nexus acts as the identity provider for a family of Side Websites. When you choose to access a Side Website from your dashboard, Nova Nexus opens it and delivers a short-lived signed authentication token (a JSON Web Token, or "JWT") directly from window to window, using your browser's native cross-window messaging with strict checks on which site is allowed to receive it — the token is never placed in a URL, so it cannot end up in browser history or a server log. The token contains only the minimal claims necessary for identification — your unique user ID, your email address, and your email verification status — and it expires automatically after a short period. Because a token remains cryptographically valid until it naturally expires, deleting your account or signing out does not instantly invalidate a token already delivered to a Side Website; it simply will not be renewed. Side Websites are expected to validate tokens cryptographically and are responsible for their own handling of any session created from one. Nova Nexus never transmits your password, and Side Websites never receive your credentials.

5. Data Storage, Security, and Firebase Infrastructure

All account data is stored using Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore, both Google Cloud products, which encrypt data at rest and in transit by default. We restrict Firestore access with security rules so a signed-in, verified user may only read and write their own profile document, only ever containing the exact fields our application defines; no user can access another user's stored data through normal use of the application. While we implement reasonable technical safeguards, no system connected to the internet can be guaranteed completely secure.

6. Bot and Abuse Protection

Nova Nexus uses Firebase App Check with Google reCAPTCHA to help confirm that requests to our systems come from our genuine application rather than automated scripts or bots. reCAPTCHA may collect hardware and software information, such as device and application data, and send it to Google for analysis; this is governed by the Google Privacy Policy and Terms of Service linked on our sign-in screen. This mechanism exists solely to protect account security and platform integrity — it is not used to track you for advertising.

7. Zero-Budget / Best-Effort Infrastructure Disclaimer

Nova Nexus currently runs as an independently funded, best-effort project on free or low-cost infrastructure tiers. We do not guarantee any specific uptime, service level, or response time, and the platform is provided on an "as available" basis. Features, storage limits, or the service itself may change, be temporarily interrupted, or be discontinued at any time, with notice provided where reasonably practical.

8. We Do Not Sell Your Data

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties, advertisers, or data brokers, under any circumstances. We do not use third-party advertising trackers. The only parties that process data on our behalf are Firebase/Google Cloud (as our infrastructure provider) and Google reCAPTCHA (for bot protection), each bound by Google's own data processing terms.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your account information for as long as your account remains active. You can permanently delete your account and profile data at any time from your dashboard's Danger Zone, without needing to contact us — this requires confirming a security phrase and re-authenticating, and takes effect immediately once confirmed. See Section 4 above regarding the limited lifetime of tokens already delivered before deletion. You may also contact us using the details below if you'd prefer we process a deletion request manually.

10. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your personal data, and to object to or restrict certain processing. You can update your username directly from your dashboard, and delete your account and data directly as described above. For any other request, contact us using the information in Section 13.

11. Children's Privacy

Nova Nexus is not directed at, and is not intended for use by, children under the age of 13 (or the minimum age required in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

12. Cookies and Local Storage

Nova Nexus does not use third-party advertising cookies. Firebase Authentication uses your browser's local or session storage (depending on whether you choose "Keep me signed in") to keep you signed in; this data stays on your device and is not shared with advertisers.

13. Changes to This Policy and Contact

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by an updated "Last Updated" date above. Questions or requests can be sent to: help.novanexus@gmail.com.
